# Start Here

This vault is the public AI Engineering knowledge graph for The AI Engineer community. Use it as a map for learning production AI systems: agents, RAG, MCP, evals, model behavior, deployment tools, and the engineering tradeoffs that connect them.

## First Path

1. Open [[Hubs/AI_Concept_Universe]] for the full map of the AI engineering curriculum.
2. Pick one domain hub:
   - [[Hubs/AI_Agents]]
   - [[Hubs/RAG]]
   - [[Hubs/Model_Context_Protocol]]
   - [[Hubs/Agent_Tool_Calling]]
   - [[Hubs/LLM_Evals]]
   - [[Hubs/Generative_AI]]
   - [[Hubs/Machine_Learning]]
   - [[Hubs/Computer_Vision]]
3. Read the linked concept notes before jumping into tools.
4. Use [[Assets/Tool_Calling_Experiment_Lab]] when you want to turn theory into agent/tool-calling experiments.
5. Use [[Assets/CLI_Playbook]] when you want to search the vault quickly from a terminal.

## What Is Public

- `Concepts/`: durable technical concepts and mental models.
- `Tools/`: concrete libraries, protocols, platforms, databases, and infrastructure primitives.
- `Hubs/`: curated maps that connect concepts into learning paths.
- `Assets/`: reusable playbooks, agents, and working templates.

## What Is Private

The local folders `Applied/`, `Sources/`, and `Daily_Notes/` are intentionally ignored by git. They are for private project blueprints, raw transcripts, client work, and personal working notes.
