## Why It Matters
- Prompt injection can subvert tool use and data boundaries unless instructions and permissions are hardened.

## Sub-Concept Map
- Injection vectors across user input and retrieved content
- Instruction hierarchy and isolation
- Tool gating and policy checks
- Content sanitization and detection

## Playbook Moves
- Separate system policy from untrusted content channels.
- Apply allowlists and policy checks before tool execution.
- Treat retrieved documents as untrusted and sanitize accordingly.
