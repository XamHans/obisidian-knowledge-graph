## Why It Matters
- Planning and reasoning strategy controls whether an agent can break complex tasks into reliable, verifiable steps.

## Sub-Concept Map
- Reactive execution for fast low-risk tasks.
- Deliberate planning for multi-step objectives.
- Reflection/self-critique loops for correction.
- Tool-mediated reasoning vs pure text reasoning.
- Stop conditions and escalation policies.

## Playbook Moves
- Choose planning depth based on risk and task complexity.
- Enforce explicit success criteria per step.
- Add verification passes before irreversible actions.
