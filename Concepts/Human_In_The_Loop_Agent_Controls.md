## Why It Matters
- Human checkpoints are required for high-risk actions where model confidence and business risk are misaligned.

## Sub-Concept Map
- Approval gates by risk tier
- Review interfaces and evidence packs
- Escalation and override policies
- Post-action audit trails

## Playbook Moves
- Classify actions by risk and require approval for irreversible operations.
- Package context, rationale, and proposed actions for fast review.
- Log reviewer decisions to improve future policy design.
