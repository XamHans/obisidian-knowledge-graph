## Why It Matters
- Unsupervised learning helps uncover hidden structure in data when labels are sparse or unavailable, which is common in early product discovery.

## Sub-Concept Map
- Clustering groups similar samples without target labels.
- Dimensionality reduction helps visualize latent structure.
- Representation learning creates reusable embeddings.
- Anomaly detection flags outliers for review workflows.
- Evaluation requires proxy metrics and qualitative checks.

## Playbook Moves
- Use unsupervised analysis before labeling to shape taxonomy.
- Validate cluster quality with downstream utility, not visuals alone.
- Feed discovered segments into supervised labeling strategy.
