# Azure AI Content Safety

## Definition
- Azure AI Content Safety is Microsoft's service for detecting and mitigating harmful or policy-violating content in AI inputs and outputs.

## Capabilities
- Safety classification for content risk categories.
- Integration into evaluation and runtime safety workflows.
- Supports risk and safety gates for GenAI systems.

## AI-300 Role
- Content Safety is a concrete safety primitive behind GenAI risk evaluation and production guardrails.
