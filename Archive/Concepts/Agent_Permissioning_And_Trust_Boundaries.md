## Why It Matters
- Agent safety depends on explicit permission scopes, least privilege, and trust boundaries across tools and data.

## Sub-Concept Map
- Least-privilege tool design
- Credential and secret isolation
- Environment and tenant boundaries
- Policy enforcement and auditing

## Playbook Moves
- Scope each tool to minimal required actions and data domains.
- Isolate credentials by environment and task class.
- Enforce policy checks before any side-effecting call.
